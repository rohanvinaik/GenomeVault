## Why AGPL-3.0?

**AGPL-3.0 prevents**:
- Companies using GenomeVault in proprietary products without contributing back
- Closed-source SaaS deployments that don't share improvements
- "embrace and extend" strategies by large corporations

**AGPL-3.0 allows**:
- Free academic and research use
- Open-source derivative works
- Community contributions
- Transparent security audits
