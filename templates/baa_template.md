## 1. DEFINITIONS

For purposes of this Agreement, the following terms shall have the meanings set forth below:

**1.1 "Business Associate"** has the same meaning as the term "business associate" in 45 CFR 164.501.

**1.2 "Covered Entity"** has the same meaning as the term "covered entity" in 45 CFR 164.501.

**1.3 "Designated Record Set"** has the same meaning as the term "designated record set" in 45 CFR 164.501.

**1.4 "Individual"** has the same meaning as the term "individual" in 45 CFR 164.501 and shall include a person who qualifies as a personal representative in accordance with 45 CFR 164.502(g).

**1.5 "Protected Health Information" or "PHI"** has the same meaning as the term "protected health information" in 45 CFR 164.501, limited to the information created or received by Business Associate from or on behalf of Covered Entity.

**2.1 General Use and Disclosure Provisions**

Business Associate may use or disclose PHI only as permitted or required by this Agreement or as Required by Law. Business Associate may not use or disclose PHI in a manner that would violate the Privacy Rule if done by Covered Entity.

**2.2 Specific Use and Disclosure Provisions**

Business Associate may use or disclose PHI for the following purposes:

a) **Genomic Analysis Services:** Processing genomic data through GenomeVault's hyperdimensional computing platform for:
   - Variant analysis and interpretation
   - Clinical risk assessment
   - Pharmacogenomic analysis
   - {{specific_services}}

b) **System Administration:** Managing and maintaining the genomic computing platform, including:
   - Data quality assurance
   - System performance monitoring
   - Technical support services

c) **Compliance Activities:** Ensuring compliance with applicable laws and regulations

Business Associate may de-identify PHI in accordance with 45 CFR 164.514(b), provided such de-identification supports the permitted purposes under Section 2.2.

## 4. BREACH NOTIFICATION

**4.1 Discovery and Notification**

Business Associate shall notify Covered Entity of any Breach of Unsecured PHI within **24 hours** of discovery, or if discovery occurs on a weekend or holiday, by the end of the first business day after discovery.

**4.2 Breach Report Contents**

The breach notification shall include:
- Description of what happened and date of breach
- Types of PHI involved
- Number of individuals affected
- Steps taken to investigate and mitigate
- Contact information for further details

**4.3 Investigation and Mitigation**

Business Associate shall:
- Investigate the breach promptly
- Implement immediate mitigation measures
- Provide detailed incident report within 5 business days
- Cooperate fully with Covered Entity's breach response

## 7. RETURN OR DESTRUCTION OF PHI

**7.1 Termination Procedures**

Upon termination of this Agreement, Business Associate shall:

a) Return all PHI and any copies to Covered Entity, or
b) If return is not feasible, destroy all PHI and provide written certification of destruction

**7.2 Destruction Methods**

When destruction is required, Business Associate shall:
- Use NIST 800-88 approved methods for electronic media
- Use cross-cut shredding for paper records
- Provide certificate of destruction with details of method used
- Destroy all backup copies and replications

**7.3 Cryptographic Erasure**

For genomic data stored with GenomeVault's encryption system, destruction may be accomplished through cryptographic erasure by destroying encryption keys, rendering data permanently unrecoverable.
